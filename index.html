MORE THAN A BEAUTIFUL FINISH

A little science.
A whole lot of shine.

Ceramic coating bonds with your paint to create a hydrophobic layer. The result: added gloss and a surface that is easier to keep clean.

Added paint protection

A protective layer between your paint and everyday environmental contamination.

Deep gloss. Mirror finish.

A rich, wet-look shine that brings out your paint’s depth and color, long after the detail.

Water beads. Dirt leaves.

Hydrophobic protection helps water, dirt, and grime slide off, making washes easier and faster.

Care for the long road

Pair the coating with careful washing and recommended maintenance to look after your finish.
